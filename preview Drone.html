Frequently asked questions

Questions clients ask before a drone survey

The right answer depends on the project specification, terrain, control, sensor and intended use. These FAQs set expectations before technical and commercial evaluation.

What information is required for a drone survey quotation?

Share the project boundary or corridor alignment, location, approximate area or length, purpose, accuracy requirement, coordinate system, expected outputs, available control data, site conditions and delivery schedule.

How accurate can a drone survey be?

Accuracy depends on sensor, flight height, ground sampling distance, RTK/PPK or GCP strategy, terrain, surface visibility, weather and processing. A project-specific accuracy plan should be agreed before mobilisation.

What is the difference between orthomosaic, DEM, DSM and DTM?

An orthomosaic is a georeferenced image. A DSM represents visible surface heights, including structures and vegetation. A DTM aims to represent bare-earth terrain. DEM is a broader elevation-model term and should be defined in the project specification.

Can Techmapperz process our existing drone imagery?

Yes. The team can review client-supplied imagery, camera metadata, flight logs, control points and coordinate information. Feasibility and achievable quality depend on coverage, overlap, blur, exposure and available georeferencing data.

Do all drone surveys require ground control points?

Not always, but control and independent checkpoints may be required depending on the accuracy target, survey system and client standard. RTK/PPK does not automatically remove the need for validation.

Which output formats can be delivered?

Typical formats include GeoTIFF, LAS/LAZ, XYZ, SHP, GeoPackage, geodatabase, DWG, DXF, KML/KMZ, OBJ and PDF reports. The final list should be confirmed in the scope.

Can drone outputs be integrated into a Web GIS dashboard?

Yes. Orthomosaics, terrain layers, mapped assets, inspection observations and progress information can be published through custom Web GIS or Mobile GIS applications.

Planning a drone survey or holding raw aerial data?

Share the project location, area or corridor length, available control, required accuracy, intended use and deliverables. Our team will review the scope and recommend a suitable survey or processing workflow.
