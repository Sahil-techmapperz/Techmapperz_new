Career FAQs

Questions candidates commonly ask.

Can freshers apply?

Yes, where a role is marked as fresher or internship. Applicants should still demonstrate basic understanding, willingness to learn and attention to detail.

Are remote positions available?

Remote or hybrid arrangements depend on the role, project requirements, data-security needs and field responsibilities.

Which GIS tools are useful?

ArcGIS, QGIS, AutoCAD, Global Mapper, Agisoft, ENVI, ERDAS, PostGIS and related tools are valuable depending on the position.

Do I need drone-pilot certification?

Only roles involving drone operations may require relevant qualifications. Processing, GIS and application roles have different requirements.

Can I apply without an active vacancy?

Yes. Join the technical talent network so your profile can be reviewed when a suitable role opens.

How will shortlisted candidates be contacted?

Shortlisted applicants should be contacted through official Techmapperz email or verified company communication channels.
